## Purpose and scope

AMRKB is an independent research project within Ecological Information, covering agricultural history, modes, systems, methods, practices and technologies. It includes traditional, Indigenous, industrial, ecological, experimental and emerging approaches across historical periods and geographical regions. Its classifications remain provisional.

Research dimensions include historical development and diffusion; philosophies and traditions; technical methods and infrastructure; relationships with soil, water, biodiversity and ecological processes; climate, terrain and place-specific suitability; and labour, land tenure and productive organisation.

Keep modes, systems, methods, practices, technologies, ecological mechanisms and indicators distinct, and record explicit relationships between them.

Corpus comprises collected sources, records and provenance. Analysis is their
explicit examination and organisation; synthesis develops arguments and
frameworks from evidence and analysis. No agricultural corpus, analytical results
or synthesis has been added by preparing these templates.

## Autonomous federation

AMRKB retains its own research, classifications, schemas, identifiers and
acceptance decisions. Neighbouring databases retain equivalent source authority.
Database Reference Methodology (DRM) develops shared methodological and
interoperability conventions; IRKB owns its independent interdisciplinary
research and synthesis. These responsibilities do not establish verified
relationships, automated exchange or operational federation services.

The current DRM planning architecture and specified methodological decisions
are approved within their stated scope. DRM-MSP 0.1.0 remains a review candidate,
not an adopted/released protocol; the pre-pilot checkpoint remains closed.
This local template does not advance a DRM phase or claim conformance.
